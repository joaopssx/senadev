# Code of Conduct

Everyone taking part in issues and pull requests here should be able to do so without harassment, whatever their background or identity.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a contribution.

## Not accepted

- Insults, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report problems to joaopdssx@gmail.com. Reports are kept private. Comments that break these rules can be removed, and repeat offenders can be blocked from the repository.
