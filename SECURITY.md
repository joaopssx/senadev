# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email joaopdssx@gmail.com with:

- what the problem is and where it happens
- steps to reproduce it
- the impact you expect

Please don't open a public issue or test against other visitors' data. I aim to reply within 7 days.

## Scope

The live site at senadev.com.br and the code in this repository, including the `/api/guestbook` and `/api/repos` endpoints.

Automated scans without a demonstrated problem, spam in the guestbook and denial of service are out of scope.
